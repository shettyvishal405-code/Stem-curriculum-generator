ENpower STEM Labs — Grade 6 Curriculum Framework
Board: CBSE/ICSE | Duration: 50 min per session | Total: 44 Sessions

Topic: What is STEM, Introduction to Technologies
Sessions: 2
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will be able to identify the four pillars of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics)
- Students will be able to name common tools and equipment in the STEM lab
- Students will understand lab safety rules and etiquette
Tangible Outcome: Understand lab components and lab etiquette

Topic: Basic Electronics
Sessions: 10
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will be able to explain the concept of electric current and voltage
- Students will be able to build simple series and parallel circuits on a breadboard
- Students will identify and describe common electronic components (LED, resistor, capacitor, diode)
- Students will build circuits with buzzers and switches
- Students will use RGB LEDs and potentiometers (sessions 9-12 only)
Tangible Outcome: Build and debug simple electronic circuits

Topic: Design Thinking
Sessions: 3
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will be able to apply the empathise-define-ideate stages of design thinking
- Students will identify real-world problems in their school or community
- Students will propose technology-based solutions using a structured canvas
Tangible Outcome: Analyse problems using Design Thinking, propose tech solutions

Topic: Working with Sensors
Sessions: 8
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will distinguish between sensors (input devices) and actuators (output devices)
- Students will describe how common sensors work (light, temperature, sound, motion)
- Students will apply if-then logic to describe simple automation scenarios
- Students will identify real-world uses of sensors in Indian daily life
Tangible Outcome: Understand input/output devices and basics of automation

Topic: Simulation (Tinkercad)
Sessions: 5
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will be able to create and test simple circuits in Tinkercad
- Students will relate simulated circuits to physical circuits built earlier
- Students will identify errors in circuit diagrams using simulation tools
Tangible Outcome: Build and simulate circuits in Tinkercad

Topic: Microcontroller Basics
Sessions: 2
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will identify and name the key components of an Arduino Uno board
- Students will distinguish between analog and digital pins
- Students will upload a basic program to Arduino using Tinkercad simulation
Tangible Outcome: Name Arduino Uno parts and use in simulation

Topic: Sensor-Based Automation using Microcontrollers
Sessions: 4
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will write simple Arduino code to read sensor data
- Students will design a basic line-following algorithm using if-else logic
- Students will simulate a sensor-based robot in Tinkercad before building physically
Tangible Outcome: Simulated robot follows a line

Topic: Robotics
Sessions: 10
Learning Outcomes:
- Students will classify robots by type and application
- Students will assemble a basic robot chassis with motors
- Students will integrate sensors into a robot system
- Students will program a robot to perform a simple task
- Students will demonstrate and present their robotic project
Tangible Outcome: Design and demonstrate a simple working robotic project
